decisions:
  - label: "Perspective"
    body: "Same top-down vantage as The Canopy, but zoomed into the site itself — arcs of white light crossing over the canopy like the ribs of something enormous. This is the ritual The Canopy is standing just outside of."

  - label: "Light"
    body: "The rings themselves are the light source — hard white, geometric, nothing diffused about them. Underneath, an orb-shaped mass glows warm and soft, carrying the same symbol that's become recurring in this series. Two kinds of light doing two different jobs: one draws the shape, one holds the meaning."

  - label: "The Figure"
    body: "Standing on one of the rings, small against the scale of the structure and the trees beneath it. Close enough to the orb to matter, far enough that the ritual clearly isn't about them personally — they're a witness to this, not the center of it."

  - label: "Scale"
    body: "The trees needed to read as impossibly tall, so the canopy had to feel like a landscape in its own right — rolling, dense, texture at every level. The ritual geometry floating above it makes the scale legible: something built large enough to be seen from a height where the forest itself looks small."

thread: "Companion to The Canopy — same world, same forest, but here the ritual is live. The teal-white light and the carved symbol tie back to the same visual language running through this series. The Survey's Vivendë keeps turning up in pieces that don't know about each other yet."
